```@raw html
  - title: Full-text papers

    link: /installation
  - title: A site
    details: Paper pages, topics, a timeline, a landscape against the group's focus areas, an arXiv watch, and a daily paper with an analysis.
    link: /serving
  - title: A tutor
    details: Per-paper chat with an agent that has read the paper and its neighbours, pitched to the reader's background.
  - title: Findings and projects
    details: Claims backed by cited evidence, and task boards that people and agents share.
    link: /work
  - title: MCP tools
    details: Agents search, read, add papers, draft findings and work on tasks, within each person's permissions.
    link: /agents
  - title: Solo or shared
    details: One person on a laptop, or a signed-in group on a server. Readers share work as datasets.
    link: /datasets

```

Named after the ゼミ (*zemi*), a Japanese university research seminar: a small group that reads and
works together and brings new members up to speed.
